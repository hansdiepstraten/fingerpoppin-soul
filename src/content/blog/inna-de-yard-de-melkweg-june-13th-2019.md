Something very special is taking place in Amsterdam on June 13th . For Reggae fans ( like we are) this bill is outstanding ! Legendary artists on stage are : Horace Andy , Ken Boothe, Cedric Myton and maybe also ( according to the InnadeYard website) The Viceroys and Judy Mowatt ! All accompanied by a big band of well experienced Jamaican musicians. Get your tickets because this will be a sell out show !  Since this gig is taking place on a thursday  Fingerpoppin Soul will be presented by our good friends Jason and Helen.
