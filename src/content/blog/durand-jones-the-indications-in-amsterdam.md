On Friday January 25 2019 we're going to see one of our current favourite bands for the third time; Durand Jones and the Indications. The gig takes place @ Paradiso Noord ( just across the water from Central Station Amsterdam) . Their new album called "American Love Call" will be out on March 1st 2019 on the wonderful Colemine label .  See y'all there !
