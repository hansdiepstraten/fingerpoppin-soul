On March 1 we have Marc Janssen as a special guest on the radio show. Marc is a very very serious soul collector and is now co-running the mighty [Everland](https://www.discogs.com/label/1249379-Everland)record label, which re-releases many ultra rare soul albums and 45s. Marc will tell us a lot about how [Everland](https://www.discogs.com/label/1249379-Everland)works and will play a number of the releases of [Everland](https://www.discogs.com/label/1249379-Everland)in the show.
