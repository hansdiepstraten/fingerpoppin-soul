On Thursday April 27 it's King's Day again in the Netherlands. Amsterdam will be heaving with street parties, music and performances all over the place. There is also the traditional street fair where everyone can sell stuff on the streets for arguably the world's largest garage sale. We will also be participating by selling our spare vinyl (45s and LPs) and the occasional CD all day on the Haarlemmerdijk in the city centre! Bargains galore!
