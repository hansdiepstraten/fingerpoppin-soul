On Friday September 27 2024 we kick off our THIRD season of the Hideaway @ the Clayton Hotel American at the Leidseplein in the centre of Amsterdam. Again the focus will be on Sweet Soul, Ballads & B-sides which will be spun by our resident team of DJs Harry van Vliet, Mark Fisher, Hans Diepstraten and new kid on the block Paul Woosnam. Start time in the new season will be 9PM running to 01AM. Here are the dates for the new season:

![](/images/hideaway-24-25-2.jpg)

Free entry as always, with a fully stocked bar, great music and lovely people. Oh we will also have special guest DJs bringing their exquisite taste to the table.
