After two years Thee Sinseers are back in the Netherlands to promote their new album Love Stories. 

Joey Quinones and his wonderful band are doing 4 gigs in Holland and Belgium:

23 september 2026: TivoliVredenburg (Pandora), Utrecht

24 september 2026: SPOT De Oosterpoort, Groningen

25 september 2026: BIRD, Rotterdam

26 september 2026: Tolhuistuin, Amsterdam

27 september 2026: Trix, Antwerpen, Belgium

Hans & Harry will be present at the Tivoli and Tolhuis gigs.
