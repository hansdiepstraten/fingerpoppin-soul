This Friday the Soul Drippin’ crew presents a night of mayhem at Club 8 at the Admiraal de Ruyterweg 56B in the west of Amsterdam. Club 8 is spacious with a large dancefloor and competitive bar prices.Apart from the resident DJs Soulfinger and Indeemixx a great list of DJs will be there to spin soul, funk, latin, and mod/beat sounds: Taco Fett, Wolfrock, DJ Sounder, and Loma Russ. The start is at 22:00 and the night runs until at least 4AM so great value for an entry fee of 6 Euros.
