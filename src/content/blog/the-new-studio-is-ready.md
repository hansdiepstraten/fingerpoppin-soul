Yes! Finally the studio at the OBA is ready and we can return to live radio again. The last live how was at the end of May and since then we had to pre-record the shows at Hans' kitchen table in The Hague. But we have made it through and starting on Thursday September 24 we will move into the brand new Salto studio at the Oosterdokskade in the center of Amsterdam next door to the Central Station. 

So tune in if you can on [https://www.salto.nl/player-radio-salto/](https://www.salto.nl/player-radio-salto/) this upcoming Thursday September 24 from 21-23 CET or from Friday morning on demand on Mixcloud.

We want to thank the fine people at Salto for their hard work to deliver this state of the art radio studio.
