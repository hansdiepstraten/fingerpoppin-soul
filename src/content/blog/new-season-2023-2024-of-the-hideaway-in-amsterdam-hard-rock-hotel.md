Good news! In september 2023 we will start our second season of The Hideaway at the Mata Lounge in the Hard Rock Hotel in the center of Amsterdam. At the Hideaway we present the best in Sweet Soul, Ballads & B-sides, spun by resident  DJ's Hans Diepstraten, Harry van Vliet, Mark Fisher and Taco Fett. Every night will also feaure (international) guest DJ's.

![](/images/mata-hideaway.jpg)

The dates for the new season are: Friday September 29 Friday November 24 Friday December 22 (the Christmas edition) Friday February 23 2024 Friday April 26 Friday June 28 We start as always at 8 PM sharp and continue at least to 00:30. Entrance is free and there will be a fully stocked bar.

If you would like to be part of the line up, drop us an email, especially if your boxes are filled with top drawer and exclusive sounds of the sweet variety. Of course there will be ample security to guard your vinyl treasures!
