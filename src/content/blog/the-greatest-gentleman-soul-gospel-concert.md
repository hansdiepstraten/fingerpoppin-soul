On Friday Aug 24th 2018 a very special concert takes place ! For one night only all the Surinam Soul legends get together for the anniversary of Gijs Winter's Fifty years in showbusiness !

Gijs recorded many ( now very collectable) singles in the Seventies. He will be joined by Brian Bijlhout, Louis Windzak ( The Dutch Otis Redding), Eddy Assan, Romeo Olmberg ( Blakka Roetoe), Ed Rust ( Master Blaster), Jules Sloote ( Combo Five), Lucien Fellclen, Alfons Wielingen ( The Needles), Henry Cumberbatch ( Falling Stones)

Where ? Wi Eegi Kerki, Kortvoort 73, Amsterdam Z.O. In Fingerpoppin Soul show no 1294, thursday Aug 23rd, we will spinning a lot of records of the above mentioned artists !    Tune in and come to the gig !
