This Saturday the Book Presentation takes place of the retrospective book on the history of legendary Amsterdam live venue Paradiso. The book was written by music writer Hester Carvalho and includes a contribution from Harry on a legendary concert by Curtis Mayfield at the Paradiso in 1990 just before his accident.
