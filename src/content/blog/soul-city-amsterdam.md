Up until some years ago Amsterdam was something of a Soul Desert with hardly any soul do's. This has changed with the arrival of several different regular events, such as the Amsterdam Soul Club (now defunkt), Amsterdam Soulful, Northern Soul Amsterdam and the regular nights at Pacific Parc; This weekend we can be found at Pacific Parc on Friday night and at the Batavia club on Saturday
