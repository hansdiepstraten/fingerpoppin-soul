Bretby Anniversary LP vol. 1 Ltd edition LP celebrating the 1st anniversary of the Bretby Soul Club; a release from the 80s I believe with then current dancers. One side of disco-soul tracks and one of northern soul Jermaine Jackson - Feel the fire (Motown) Nice lp by the underrated brother of Michael Jackson. Best track is the two-step "You need to be loved" Bobby Thurston - You got what it takes (Jupiter) Disco-heavy alum by Bobby. Highlight (and the reason for playing it) is the version of "Sitting in the park" Orquesta Suprema - The Salsa Family (Sound Triangle) Florida salsa from 1980; very soulful and good songs
