New era, new look

SALTO has stood for open access for more than 40 years and makes its channels available to all Amsterdammers. With the new facilities in the OBA, we're opening ourselves up even more to the public, with greater visibility for the creators and the facilities. This way, anyone visiting the OBA can get acquainted with SALTO and its countless media makers.

Radio studios

There are two spaces being set up as radio studios with the most modern equipment. The old DHD audio consoles had been running for a combined 20 years. They are now being replaced with modern DHD consoles.

There will be one large radio studio, in which visual radio can also be recorded. In addition, there is one compact radio studio with the technical equipment housed separately.
