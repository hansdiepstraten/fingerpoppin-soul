Our fifth season of The Hideaway starts this October in the **Marquee Room**
at the **Clayton Hotel American** on the Leidsekade. Same spirit, same free
entry, same fully stocked bar.

Resident DJs spin vinyl only — sweet soul and ballads building up to mid-tempo
steppers, Philly, 80s grooves and Lovers Rock. Doors 20:00, on until 01:00.

**The dates for 2026/27:**

- Saturday 3 October 2026, special guest Ishmael Ibrahim
- Saturday 21 November 2026
- Saturday 23 January 2027, special guests from Belgium  Raphael van Weyenbergh and Kris van Beethoven
- Saturday 20 March 2027
- Saturday 22 May 2027
- Saturday 19 June 2027

Guest DJs are announced ahead of each night — see [The Hideaway](/the-hideaway)
for the full line-up. If your boxes are filled with top-drawer sweet soul and
you'd like to play, [drop us a line](/#contact).
