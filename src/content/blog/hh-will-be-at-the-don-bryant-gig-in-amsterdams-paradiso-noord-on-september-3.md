On Sunday September 3 soul legend Don Bryant will be performing at the Tolhuistuin aka Paradiso Noord in Amsterdam. Doors open at 8PM, showtime at 9PM. Hans & Harry will be there of course! The DJ's at the event will be Amsterdam Soulful's very own Helen Waddington & Jan Harris so be early!
