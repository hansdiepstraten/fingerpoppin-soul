This weekend Hans & Harry will be at the famous [Porretta Soul Festival](http://www.porrettasoulfestival.it/) in the beautiful town of Porretta Terme in Italy near Bologna. This year is edition number 30! Acts appearing are amongst others: Carla & Vaneese Thomas, Willie Hightower, Wee Willie Walker, Ricky Fante, and Vasti Jackson.
