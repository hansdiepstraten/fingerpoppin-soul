We're really looking forward to this gig @Paradiso, Bovenzaal on Monday March 18th 2019 ! . Ural started his recording career in 1964 (!) with vocal group The Monterays. In 1968 he released his only solo album on Revue Records called "Can you dig it,Live". On that album is one of the deepest Soul songs you'll ever hear "Pain is the name of your game". His current band is named after this song. in 2018 they released a stunning new album called "The Right Time". Ural will be accompanied by his 7 man band from Portland , Oregon including a 3 man horn section ! See ya all there !!!
