On Sunday July 16 Paradiso hosts a soul legend from the James Brown stable, Martha High. She was a backing singer for James Brown for many years and was also a successful solo artist. Her latest album “Singing for the good times” was released last year on an Italian label. Martha will be performing with a band featuring several of the original JB’s!
