Next week the Lost Soul weekender takes place in Amsterdam at the Batavia in the center of town, close to the Central Station.

![](/images/lostsoul.jpg)

The weekender consists of TWO soul nights on the Friday and the Saturday night (both nights fully sold out!) and on Saturday afternoon there is a free Chill Out session at the Batavia with some of Europe's leading soul collectors and DJs playing mellow and deep chillout tunes. Free and open entry for all for the afternoon, starting at 2PM.

On Thursday night the Fingerpoppin Soul radio show will have top UK soul DJ Dave Thorley as special guest. Dave is DJing at the weekender and will start off proceedings on Thursday on the radio show where he gets to play 90 minutes of his choices. This will be great stuff!
